# Playbook: LinkedIn and social posts

**Use when:** he says "post it", "give me a write-up for X", or forwards a comment to answer.

## Rules
- "Post X on LinkedIn with a write-up" is permission to post once. Check his feed first; never repost; never like, comment, share or follow (except the first comment on his own new post when asked).
- Use native video uploads on LinkedIn; put the YouTube link in the first comment.
- Write-ups: short, plain, first person, honest labels ("Concept ad", "Made with AI", "Created by Olatunde Clement"), portfolio link, a few relevant hashtags (check for duplicates).
- "Give me a write-up for <platform>": give copyable text with the link and channel name. He posts on his own accounts unless he says otherwise.
- Comment replies: draft short, warm, copyable replies.
- After picking an @mention, read the editor text back (a space can be eaten).

## Per platform notes
X: keep under the limit (a link counts as 23 characters). TikTok and Instagram: put the YouTube link in the bio. Facebook: slightly longer story is fine.
