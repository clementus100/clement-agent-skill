# AFONJA series: production rules
- Yoruba dialogue, English subtitles, dramatised history. Terms: Ààrẹ (my lord), Balógun (captain or commander). Spellings: Àfọ̀njá, Ṣẹ́hù Àlímì, Ṣọ́lágbérù.
- Afonja look is locked: red war smock, cowries, snail shells, tira amulets, two-layer cap, long sword, LEFT-cheek scar. Alimi: white turban and robe.
- Do not show Alimi personally killing Afonja. Part 2 ends with "TO BE CONTINUED". Part 3 pointers: Afonja's fall c.1823, Solagberu eliminated, Alimi's son Abdus-Salam, Sokoto Caliphate.
- Voices (ElevenLabs eleven_v4, locked): Narrator Ola; Afonja Steven; Aole Victor Hopo; Alimi Hassan; Captain and Chief Commander Moyo; Palace Chief and Second Commander Chris Razor; Solagberu Taiwo; Warrior 1 Tayo; Warrior 2 Uche. Rejected: Gemnda, Prince.
- Shots: locked-off steady camera, faces toward camera, each person moves independently with varied natural gestures, no smoke across faces, speakers show clear lip and jaw movement; spoken lines are lip-synced, or covered by wide or reaction shots when they cannot be.
- New faces are made fresh; never reuse faces from Ijaye, Ogunmola or Kurunmi.
- End card: dramatisation note, Part 2 covers Afonja's fall c.1823 to 1824, "Created by Olatunde Clement", "Made with AI".
- Channel videos are exported upscaled to 4K (3840x2160) and delivered by download link; tell him it is upscaled, not native.
