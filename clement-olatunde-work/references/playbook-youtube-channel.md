# Playbook: YouTube channel (Echoes of Africa History, @EchoesOfHistoryNG)

**Use when:** uploads, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, branding, monetisation.

- Tab never logs into his Google account; it prepares assets and copyable text, he applies them in Studio.
- Per video: title, description (AI-made note, "Created by Clement Olatunde", links), tags, thumbnail (code-drawn), chapters if long.
- Branding: style 2 (dark glass, glowing West Africa map): banner, logo, 150 px watermark.
- Monetisation (from YouTube's help pages): early level needs 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days and 3,000 watch hours (or 3M Shorts views); ad revenue needs 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours in 12 months (or 10M Shorts views); also 2-Step Verification and no strikes. Check the current pages before quoting.
- Disclose AI use and keep the content clearly original and transformative.
