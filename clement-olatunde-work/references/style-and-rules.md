# Style and rules for ads and motion design
- Voice-over: very concise, calm, not AI-sounding; locked narrator Ola (ElevenLabs). Time on-screen phrases to the speech.
- Music: none copyrighted; synthesize drum beds, whooshes and thunder, or use CC-licensed tracks and credit them.
- Echoes of Africa History identity: style 2 (dark glass, glowing West Africa map). The gold 3D thunder look was rejected and is dead.
- VANTA direction (glass cards around a phone) was accepted for VANTA only; the Pixel ad is phone-interface led.
- Watermark: "CO Creativity", bottom-left, about 60 percent opacity.
- Concept ad labels: "Concept ad, fictional brand" or "Concept only, not affiliated with <brand>".
- Photo retouch: keep identity, face shape and rich natural skin tone; avoid grey or orange results.
- Links for deliverables: surge.sh or vercel, not tab.bot.
