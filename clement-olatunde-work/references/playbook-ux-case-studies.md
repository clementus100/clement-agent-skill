# Playbook: UX case studies and app screens

**Use when:** new app concept, screens, case study, or portfolio card.

## Workflow
1. Frame: who, problem, hypothesis (mark illustrative scenarios as illustrative).
2. Screens: design mobile-first, full set (Complat 60, StepWise 30 plus cover, Calmly), consistent system.
3. Case study page: overview, problem, research or assumptions, solution, key screens, what is next (do not claim testing that was not done).
4. Portfolio: add a card with the right id in `index.html`, media under `media/`, commit to `main` (auto-deploys on Vercel), then verify desktop and a 390 px mobile view.
5. Optional promo video from the screens.

## Pitfalls
Do not state results or users that do not exist. Keep the UX certificate verify link where it was added.
