# Playbook: concept ads and motion design

**Use when:** Clement asks for a product ad, brand promo, motion-graphics piece or UI ad.

## Inputs to get (ask at most one question)
Brand or product name (fictional unless he names a real one), format (16:9, 9:16, or both), length, voice-over or music-only.

## Workflow
1. Write the concept in 3 lines (hook, middle, end card). Share only if he asked to review first.
2. Draw visuals in code (PIL, numpy, OpenCV; fonts Anton, Bebas, Archivo Black, Playfair, Inter, Space Mono) or generate real motion clips. Do not use stills with pan or zoom as "video".
3. Voice-over: concise, calm, human. Locked narrator Ola. Time every on-screen phrase to the speech.
4. Sound: synthesize drum bed, whoosh and thunder, or use a licensed track and credit it. No copyrighted music. Avoid spy or thriller tracks.
5. Export 1080p H.264 + AAC. Make a small WhatsApp version (under 16 MB) and a full-quality file.
6. Watch frames and listen (levels and transcript) before sending.

## Quality bar
- Not flat, not simple, not old-looking: modern, sharp, minimal type with weight and fast motion.
- Glass cards around a phone worked for VANTA only; vary the layout per brand.
- Labels on screen: "Concept ad, fictional brand". Watermark "CO Creativity" bottom-left at about 60 percent.

## Known pitfalls
- Gold 3D thunder and holographic looks were rejected as old-looking.
- Small text becomes unreadable at 150 px (watermarks, logos): test small sizes.
- Fonts and text clipping on cards: check every card at full size.
- Never claim 4K unless the pixels are 3840x2160; say upscaled when it is.

## Delivery
Short caption first, then the video, then the full file as a document. Name weak spots honestly if asked or if they would change his decision.
