# Clement Olatunde: work and working style

Clement Olatunde is a UX/UI designer, motion designer and filmmaker in Akure, Nigeria. Links: GitHub https://github.com/clementus100, portfolio https://clementolatunde-portfolio.vercel.app/, LinkedIn https://www.linkedin.com/in/clementolatunde, YouTube "Echoes of Africa History" (@EchoesOfHistoryNG, https://www.youtube.com/@EchoesOfHistoryNG). His AI worker, Tab, produced most of the pieces below at his direction. Dates are 2026. This file is built from Tab's notes and from his public repos; items marked (planned) are not finished.

## Rules that apply to everything (his words, applied every time)
- Label honestly: concept or spec ads say "Concept ad, fictional brand" or "not affiliated with <brand>". AI-made work says "Made with AI". Credit line: "Created by Olatunde Clement".
- Real motion in every video shot (no Ken Burns, no reversed clips). Smooth transitions. Fix flagged glitches. Keep scenes uncongested; every person moves individually.
- Yoruba films: all dialogue in Yoruba with English subtitles; proofread Yoruba; keep character looks and voices locked once chosen.
- No copyrighted music: use licensed or synthesized sound and credit the source.
- Design should look modern, sharp and minimal, with weight and fast motion. Not flat, not old-looking.
- Keep replies and copy short and plain. Give honest numbered options when he asks for feedback.
- Never put tokens, passwords, bank or ID details in any repo, post or file.
- Add his portfolio link to every application. Do not take tests or interviews for him.

## Before you finish any task
1. Does it follow the labels rule (concept, made with AI, created by)?
2. Did you check the output yourself (watch frames, listen, open the link)?
3. Did you say plainly what is unchecked or imperfect?
4. Did you keep secrets and private details out of anything public?
